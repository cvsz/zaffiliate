zaffiliate — Affiliate Automation OS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-26 · Applies to the zaffiliate service at zaffiliate.zeaz.dev and its TikTok Shop integration.

1. Who we are

zaffiliate is a self-hosted affiliate commerce automation platform operated by the zaffiliate project owner ("we", "us"). Merchants and creators connect marketplace accounts (today: TikTok Shop sandbox and production programs) so affiliate collaboration data, content publishing and commission tracking can be automated under their control.

2. Data we process

  • Account identifiers: your user id, login subject, and workspace (tenant) membership created by your operator.
  • TikTok Shop connection data: seller/open-id, shop name and region, granted scopes (currently seller.affiliate_collaboration.read, seller.affiliate_collaboration.write), and OAuth access/refresh tokens. Tokens are stored server-side only, referenced through our secret manager; they are never delivered to browsers or client bundles.
  • Affiliate commerce data: products, offers with price snapshots, affiliate links, clicks, orders/conversions, commissions and refunds reported by connected platforms.
  • Click attribution pseudonyms: redirect clicks record a salted SHA-256 hash of IP + user-agent. Raw IP addresses are not stored in attribution records.
  • Operational records: audit events of administrative and autonomous actions, workflow/publication job state, webhook delivery payloads needed for reconciliation.
  • Generated content: briefs, hooks, scripts and copy produced for your campaigns, including provenance metadata.

3. Why we process it

  • To provide the service you (or your operator) configured: catalog sync, link generation, publishing automation, analytics and payouts reconciliation.
  • To enforce security: tenant isolation, rate limiting, webhook signature verification and replay protection, tamper-evident audit logging.
  • To comply with platform requirements, including TikTok Shop app review and developer policies.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use marketplace data for advertising outside the service.

4. TikTok Shop integration specifics

When a seller connects a TikTok Shop, we request only the scopes listed above and only use them to deliver affiliate features the seller enabled. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • In-app: Connections → Disconnect (calls the platform revocation and deletes stored tokens and the account link immediately).
  • API: POST /api/v1/oauth/tiktokshop/disconnect with your user identity.
  • Platform-side: removing authorization in TikTok Shop settings causes our refresh lifecycle to mark the connection REAUTH_REQUIRED and stop all data pulls for that shop.

5. Retention and deletion

Business records are kept while your workspace is active. Audit events are append-only and retained per the operations retention policy to preserve integrity. On verified deletion request we: disconnect all platform links, delete stored tokens, delete workspace business data, and retain only the minimum audit entries required for security/compliance, pseudonymized where possible.

6. Security

Tenant isolation is enforced at the application and database layers (row-level security). Secrets are held in a server-side secret manager and redacted from logs. Transport is HTTPS end-to-end. See SECURITY.md and THREAT-MODEL.md in the source repository for controls and residual risks.

7. Contact

Privacy questions, export or deletion requests: privacy@zeaz.dev. General support: support@zeaz.dev.
