# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Positive behavior includes welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or derogatory comments, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Abuse or harassment should be reported privately to project maintainers through an appropriate private channel. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
